The house file

A perfume house that shows its drawings

Nouryah was founded in 2019 in a second-floor atelier in Gulberg, Lahore, by two perfumers who had spent a decade writing formulas nobody outside the lab was allowed to read. The house exists to invert that: every composition is published with its materials, its concentration, and an honest account of how it behaves over ten hours on skin.

Nine fragrances, no seasonal churn. Each is blended in 400-flacon batches, macerated for six weeks, then filled, labelled and boxed by hand. When a batch sells out it is remade to the same formula — not reformulated.
